There are three primary actions you can apply to items.

### `remove`
The nuclear option. This completely scrubs the item from the game context based on your global config.
* Removes from Creative Tabs, EMI/JEI/REI, Loot tables, and Villager trades.
* Deletes from player inventories and world drops.

[View 'Basic Removal' Examples](/project/reliable-remover/docs/reliable-remover/examples/basic-removal)

### `remove_interactions`
Prevents the player from "using" the item (Right-Click).
* **Effect**: Cancels `useItem`, `useOn`, and `interactLivingEntity` events.
* **Use Case**: Useful if you want an item to exist for crafting but not be usable (e.g., banning a specific wand, tool, or bucket).

[View 'Interaction Blocking' Examples](/project/reliable-remover/docs/reliable-remover/examples/interaction-blocking)

### `remove_attacks`
Prevents the player from attacking entities while holding the specified item.
* **Effect**: Cancels the attack event.
* **Use Case**: Disable specific weapons or protect specific entities (like Villagers) from being hurt.

[View 'Combat Restrictions' Examples](/project/reliable-remover/docs/reliable-remover/examples/combat-restrictions)
