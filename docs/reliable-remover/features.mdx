**Remove items completely.** A removed item disappears from:

* Creative tabs
* [EMI](https://modrinth.com/mod/emi), [JEI](https://modrinth.com/mod/jei) and [RRV](https://modrinth.com/mod/rrv), including info pages
* Recipes and tags (through [Reliable Recipes](https://modrinth.com/mod/reliable-recipes))
* Player inventories, chests, and items on the ground
* Loot tables and villager trades
* Mob armor and held items

**Or only block certain things.** Keep the item, but stop players from right-clicking it, attacking with it, placing it, and [more](/project/reliable-remover/latest/docs/reliable-remover/removal-actions).

**Pick items however you want.** By ID, by tag, by mod, by regex, or by item data like damage or enchantments.

**Add conditions.** Only in the Nether, only on zombies, everything from a mod except one item.

**Remove enchantments, potions and status effects**, or swap them for something else.

**Edit in-game.** Settings screen via [YACL](https://modrinth.com/mod/yacl), and a dev mode that lets you delete items straight from EMI or RRV.
